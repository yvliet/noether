# Canvas

Infinite visual whiteboard to lay out notes, drag connector arrows, and group ideas spatially.

## 1. Canvas Basics
---

Canvases are saved as `.canvas` JSON files directly inside your vault. You can create one from the sidebar or press `Ctrl+Shift+C`.

- **Add Notes**: Drag any `.md` file from the sidebar onto the canvas. It renders a live, editable preview of the note. Edits made inside the card sync directly to the original markdown file on disk.
- **Add Text Cards**: Double-click anywhere on the canvas grid (or press `Alt+1`) to create a quick sticky note.
- **Connect Cards**: Hover over any card edge to reveal the connector dot, then drag an arrow to another card.
- **Group Cards**: Press `Alt+2` and drag a rectangle around multiple cards to group them with a custom title and background tint.

## 2. Navigation & Shortcuts
---

- **Pan**: Hold `Space` and drag, or use middle-click drag.
- **Zoom**: `Ctrl + Mouse Wheel` zooms toward your cursor. Press `Ctrl+0` to reset to 100%, or `Shift+1` to fit all cards into view.
- **Duplicate**: Press `Ctrl+D` to duplicate selected cards.
- **Resizing**: Drag card handles to resize. Holding `Shift` while resizing scales proportionally from the center.
