# Outline

Outline extracts the heading hierarchy (`#`, `##`, `###`) of the active note and displays a clickable table of contents in the sidebar.

## 1. Overview
---

For long notes, research articles, or project plans, the Outline pane gives you a bird's-eye view of your document structure and lets you jump to any section with a single click.

## 2. Open the Outline Pane
---

1. Open a document containing Markdown headings.
2. Click the **Outline** icon in the right sidebar rail.
3. The pane lists all headings indented according to their level (`H1` through `H6`).

## 3. Navigate and Collapse Headings
---

- **Jump to Section**: Click any heading in the outline to smooth-scroll the editor directly to that section.
- **Collapse Tree**: Click the chevron next to parent headings (`H1`, `H2`) to fold or unfold sub-sections in the outline.
- **Active Section Highlighting**: As you scroll through the note in the editor, the Outline dynamically highlights the current reading position.

## 4. Keyboard Shortcuts & Commands
---

| Shortcut / Command | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Ctrl+Shift+O` | Toggle Outline sidebar panel |
| `Command Palette → Outline: Focus outline` | Open and focus document outline |

> [!NOTE]
> The Outline updates instantly as you edit headings in the document.
