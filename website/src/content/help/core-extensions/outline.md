# Outline

Clickable table of contents in the sidebar to jump between headings in long notes.

## 1. Using the Outline
---

Press `Ctrl+Shift+O` or click the outline list icon in the right sidebar rail to view the active note's heading structure:

- **Jump to Section**: Click any heading to scroll the editor directly to that block.
- **Fold Sub-sections**: Click the chevron next to parent headings (`H1`, `H2`) to collapse or expand nested sections in the outline.
- **Scroll Tracking**: As you scroll through the document, the outline highlights your current reading position.
- **Real-Time Updates**: Changes you make to headings in the editor update the outline immediately.
