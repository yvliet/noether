# Community Marketplace

Browse, install, and update community extensions and themes directly inside Noether.

## 1. Installing Extensions
---

Open **Settings (`Ctrl+,`) → Community Extensions** and click **Browse Community Extensions**:

- **Search & Discover**: Filter by author, name, or keywords (e.g. `calendar`, `git`, `formatter`).
- **One-Click Install**: Click on an extension card to read its description and permissions, then click **Install**.
- **Instant Activation**: Click **Enable** to activate the extension immediately without restarting the desktop app. Bundles are evaluated directly in memory.

## 2. Managing & Updating
---

- **Updates**: Noether periodically checks the community registry for newer versions. When an update is published, an **Update** badge appears next to the extension in Settings.
- **Per-Vault Control**: Extensions can be enabled or disabled individually per vault.
- **Uninstalling**: Click the trash icon in Settings to remove an extension's files from `.noether/extensions/`.
